2. ጽሑፍን ወደ ድምጽ ማመንጫ (TTS)

Architecture: Tacotron2 (BiLSTM Encoder + Location-Sensitive Attention + PostNet) + HiFi-GAN Vocoder

Features: 22050Hz Audio, Grapheme-to-Phoneme (G2P), Multi-Scale Discriminators
